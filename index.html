Features

Everything the genome grind needs

Six focused systems built around how Evolve a Cell actually plays — from the first split to the final prestige loop.

✂️

Auto-Split Engine

Splits cells the moment they reach your target threshold, with adjustable timing so your colony never idles and never over-splits.

🧬

Gene Mutation Presets

Save named mutation builds — speed, size, efficiency — and swap between them with one click instead of re-picking genes every run.

💾

Freeplay Genome Save / Load

Export your freeplay genome to a file and reload it later. Keep experimental builds safe and share them between machines.

⏭️

Stage Skip Logic

Automatically skip cleared stages and push straight into the next one, cutting the repeated early-game clicks to zero.

⚡

Instant Prestige

Triggers prestige at your chosen threshold and restarts the loop instantly, so long prestige chains run unattended.

🪟

Native Windows App

Ships as a small standalone executable for Windows 10 and 11 — no runtime installs, no background services, no clutter.
